Ministry Tracker

Return visits, Bible studies and ministry hours. Sign in with the Google account you use for the app.

Open the app

Now add this page to your home screen. Tapping the new icon opens the app straight away.

iPhone

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep the name Ministry and tap Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen, then Add.

If you already have an older icon for the app, you can delete it.
